## 3. Service Pages

### Service 1: PAN Card Services
**H1:** Apply for PAN Card Online
**Meta Title:** Apply for PAN Card Online | Business & Individual Services
**Meta Description:** Get your Permanent Account Number (PAN) correctly. We process new applications, name corrections, and mandatory Aadhaar linking to keep you compliant.

**Direct Answer:**
A Permanent Account Number (PAN) is a ten-digit unique identifier mandatory for all taxpayers in India. You must have a PAN to file returns, open a business bank account, or conduct transactions above ₹2.5 Lakhs `<!-- VERIFY -->`. 

**Body Copy:**
Your PAN is the anchor for your entire financial identity with the government. Without it, you cannot legally operate a business or invest. We handle the paperwork to ensure your details match across all government databases perfectly.

If your name or date of birth is incorrect on your current card, you will face mismatches when filing taxes. A mismatch stops the system from accepting your return. We file the exact forms required to correct your existing data.

For individuals, linking Aadhaar (your biometric identity) with your PAN is now a statutory requirement. If they are not linked, your PAN becomes inoperative. We manage the linking process and resolve demographic mismatches so your accounts remain active.

**Who Needs This:**
- New business owners and partners.
- Individuals needing name or date of birth corrections.
- Taxpayers whose PAN has become inoperative due to unlinked Aadhaar.

**Penalty/Risk:**
If your PAN becomes inoperative, you cannot file your income tax returns. Any pending refunds will be stopped. Additionally, tax deducted at source (TDS) will be deducted at a much higher rate of 20% `<!-- VERIFY -->` instead of your standard rate.

### Service 2: GST Registration
**H1:** GST Registration Services Online
**Meta Title:** GST Registration Services Online | Secure Your GSTIN
**Meta Description:** Register your business for GST. We handle the documentation, submit the application, and secure your GSTIN so you can legally collect tax and claim ITC.

**Direct Answer:**
GST registration is mandatory for businesses crossing the ₹20 Lakh `<!-- VERIFY -->` turnover threshold. We prepare the application, submit your proof of address, and secure your GST Identification Number (GSTIN) usually within 7 working days `<!-- VERIFY -->`.

**Body Copy:**
Operating without a GST registration when you are legally required to have one is tax evasion. We evaluate your business structure, determine your exact threshold requirements, and file the application under the correct jurisdiction. 

The registration process requires specific documentation, including proof of business address (like a rental agreement or utility bill) and identity proofs for all directors or partners. We review these documents before submission to prevent the department from rejecting the application due to clerical errors.

Once registered, you can legally collect GST from your customers. More importantly, you can claim Input Tax Credit (ITC)—the tax you have already paid on your business expenses. We ensure your registration is active so your cash flow is protected.

**Who Needs This:**
- Businesses crossing the mandatory turnover threshold.
- E-commerce sellers (registration is mandatory regardless of turnover).
- Businesses buying goods across state lines (inter-state commerce).

**Penalty/Risk:**
Failing to register for GST when liable attracts a penalty of 10% of the tax due or ₹10,000 `<!-- VERIFY -->`, whichever is higher. 100% penalty applies in cases of deliberate evasion. Furthermore, you cannot claim any input tax credit for the period you operated unregistered.

### Service 3: Entity Formation
**H1:** Company Registration Services
**Meta Title:** Company Registration Services | Partnerships & Trusts
**Meta Description:** Legally form your business structure. We draft and register partnership deeds, joint ventures, societies, and trusts with precise legal documentation.

**Direct Answer:**
Selecting the right legal structure determines your tax liability and personal risk. We draft the founding documents and register your Partnership, Trust, Society, or Joint Venture with the Registrar, ensuring your business is legally recognized.

**Body Copy:**
A handshake is not a business structure. To protect yourself from liability, you need a formalized entity. For partnerships, we draft the Partnership Deed—the document outlining profit sharing, capital contribution, and dispute resolution. We then register this deed with the Registrar of Firms.

If you are operating a non-profit, we handle Society and Trust registrations. This involves drafting the Trust Deed or Memorandum of Association, defining the charitable objectives clearly so you can later apply for tax exemptions under Sections 12A and 80G.

For complex, project-specific collaborations, we draft Joint Venture agreements. These documents define the exact scope, financial input, and exit strategy for two businesses working together, preventing costly legal battles when the project ends.

**Who Needs This:**
- Two or more individuals starting a business together.
- Philanthropists setting up a charitable trust or NGO.
- Companies collaborating on a specific government or private contract.

**Penalty/Risk:**
Operating an unregistered partnership means you cannot file a suit in any court to enforce a right arising from a contract. If a dispute occurs, you have no legal standing. Incorrectly drafted trust deeds result in the immediate denial of tax-exempt status, making all donations taxable.

### Service 4: Income Tax Returns Filing
**H1:** Online ITR Filing Services
**Meta Title:** Online ITR Filing Services | Direct Tax Compliance
**Meta Description:** Accurate Income Tax Return filing for individuals and businesses. We calculate liabilities, apply deductions, and file your ITR safely before the deadline.

**Direct Answer:**
Every business and individual exceeding the basic exemption limit must file an Income Tax Return (ITR). We calculate your exact tax liability, apply all eligible statutory deductions, and file the correct ITR form before the July 31 deadline `<!-- VERIFY -->`.

**Body Copy:**
Filing an ITR is not just about paying tax; it is a declaration of your financial health. We analyze your bank statements, investment proofs, and accounting ledgers to determine your exact taxable income. We ensure you are not paying more than the law requires.

The income tax department uses different forms depending on your income source. Filing the wrong form (like using ITR-1 when you have business income) renders your return defective. We select the correct form—ITR-3, ITR-4, or ITR-6—based on your specific business structure.

Before filing, we reconcile your data with Form 26AS (your annual tax statement) and the Annual Information Statement (AIS). The department already knows what you earned. We ensure the numbers we submit match the government's records precisely to prevent scrutiny notices.

**Who Needs This:**
- Proprietors and salaried individuals with complex investments.
- Partnership firms and Limited Liability Partnerships (LLPs).
- Businesses requiring a tax audit under Section 44AB.

**Penalty/Risk:**
Filing your return after the due date attracts a late fee of up to ₹5,000 `<!-- VERIFY -->` under Section 234F. You also lose the right to carry forward business losses to offset future profits, permanently costing your business money.

### Service 5: TDS Returns Filing
**H1:** TDS Return Filing Consultant
**Meta Title:** TDS Return Filing Consultant | Timely 24Q & 26Q Filings
**Meta Description:** Avoid TDS penalties. We calculate, reconcile, and file your quarterly TDS returns for salaries, rent, and contractor payments.

**Direct Answer:**
If you deduct Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) from payments like employee salaries or contractor fees, you must file quarterly returns. We reconcile your deductions and file Forms 24Q or 26Q before the quarterly statutory deadlines.

**Body Copy:**
When you pay an employee or a vendor, the law requires you to act as a tax collector for the government. You deduct a percentage of the payment and deposit it using a challan. But depositing the money is only half the job. 

You must file a quarterly TDS return linking the deposited money to the specific PAN of the person you paid. If you fail to file this return, your employees and vendors cannot claim credit for the tax you deducted, leading to disputes and damaged business relationships.

We process your payroll and vendor data, cross-check the PAN details, and generate the required TDS returns. Once the government processes the return, we generate Form 16 and Form 16A certificates so you can distribute them to your payees on time.

**Who Needs This:**
- Employers running a payroll.
- Businesses paying rent exceeding ₹2.4 Lakhs `<!-- VERIFY -->` annually.
- Companies hiring professional consultants or contractors.

**Penalty/Risk:**
The penalty for late filing of a TDS return is ₹200 per day `<!-- VERIFY -->` under Section 234E, continuing until the return is filed. Failing to deduct TDS at all means that specific business expense will be disallowed, artificially inflating your profit and your own income tax bill.

### Service 6: GST Returns Filing
**H1:** GST Return Filing Services
**Meta Title:** GST Return Filing Services | GSTR-1 & GSTR-3B
**Meta Description:** Keep your GST compliance in check. We handle monthly reconciliations, GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, and Annual Returns so you never lose Input Tax Credit.

**Direct Answer:**
Registered businesses must declare their sales and purchases regularly. We reconcile your inward supplies with government data, calculate your exact tax liability, and file your GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B accurately to prevent notices and protect your cash flow.

**Body Copy:**
GST relies entirely on matching data. Your GSTR-1 records your sales. Your GSTR-3B records your tax payment. If the numbers in these two forms do not match exactly, the department's automated systems will flag your profile and issue a notice. We ensure absolute parity before hitting submit.

The most critical part of GST filing is claiming Input Tax Credit (ITC). You can only claim ITC if your suppliers have filed their returns properly. We download your GSTR-2B (an auto-generated statement of your purchases) and reconcile it against your accounting software to identify missing credits.

At the end of the financial year, businesses must file GSTR-9, the annual return. This requires consolidating twelve months of data and declaring any missed liabilities. We manage this massive data exercise to close your financial year cleanly.

**Who Needs This:**
- Any business holding an active GSTIN.
- Exporters claiming GST refunds.
- Businesses registered under the Composition Scheme (filing CMP-08).

**Penalty/Risk:**
Late filing of GSTR-3B attracts a late fee of ₹50 per day `<!-- VERIFY -->`. More dangerously, if you claim ITC that does not appear in your GSTR-2B, the department will demand you reverse the credit and pay it back with 18% `<!-- VERIFY -->` annual interest.

### Service 7: Appeals (Income Tax & GST)
**H1:** Income Tax Appeal Filing (CIT-A)
**Meta Title:** Income Tax & GST Appeal Filing | Tax Litigation Defense
**Meta Description:** Defend against unjust tax demands. We draft the legal response, compile evidence, and represent your case before Appellate authorities.

**Direct Answer:**
If you receive an unjust tax demand or a notice of penalty, you have the statutory right to appeal. We draft the formal legal response, compile the necessary evidence, and represent your case before the Commissioner of Appeals or the GST Tribunal.

**Body Copy:**
An assessment order demanding additional tax is not the final word. Assessors make errors, misinterpret the law, or ignore submitted evidence. When this happens, paying the unjust demand damages your working capital. The correct procedure is to file a formal appeal.

Appeals require strict adherence to procedural law. You cannot simply write a letter explaining your side. We draft the Statement of Facts and the Grounds of Appeal—highly specific legal documents that cite previous tribunal judgements and sections of the law to dismantle the assessing officer's arguments.

Time is the most critical factor. You typically have only 30 days from the date you receive an order to file an appeal. We act immediately to secure a stay of demand, preventing the department from freezing your bank accounts while the appeal is pending.

**Who Needs This:**
- Taxpayers who received a high-pitched assessment order.
- Businesses whose GST registration was cancelled abruptly.
- Individuals facing penalties for concealed income.

**Penalty/Risk:**
If you miss the 30-day `<!-- VERIFY -->` window to file an appeal, the tax demand becomes final and payable. The department can initiate recovery proceedings, which include attaching your bank accounts, seizing assets, and halting your business operations completely.

### Service 8: Import and Export Licence & Compliances
**H1:** Apply for IEC Code Online
**Meta Title:** Apply for IEC Code Online | Import Export Licence Services
**Meta Description:** Start trading internationally. We process your Import Export Code (IEC) application and handle ongoing DGFT compliances for your business.

**Direct Answer:**
An Import Export Code (IEC) is a mandatory 10-digit code required to ship goods into or out of India. We process your application with the Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT) so you can clear customs and start trading internationally.

**Body Copy:**
You cannot move commercial goods across the Indian border without an IEC. It is the primary identification number recognized by the Customs department. We compile your bank certificates, entity proofs, and address documents to file a flawless application with the DGFT.

Once issued, an IEC is permanently valid. However, the law requires you to update or confirm your IEC details electronically every year between April and June. We manage this annual compliance process so your code is never deactivated by the government.

Beyond the initial registration, exporters must navigate complex schemes like the Export Promotion Capital Goods (EPCG) scheme or the Advance Authorisation scheme to save on duties. We advise on these compliances, ensuring you receive the maximum legal benefit from your export activities.

**Who Needs This:**
- Manufacturers exporting goods outside India.
- Traders importing goods for domestic sale.
- Service providers exporting software or consultancy services.

**Penalty/Risk:**
Attempting to import or export commercial goods without an active IEC will result in your shipment being seized at the port by Customs. If you fail to update your IEC annually, the DGFT will deactivate it, instantly halting your ability to clear any shipments.

## 4. Lexicon Table: Translating Tax Jargon

| Tax Jargon | How a Normal Person Says It |
| :--- | :--- |
| Input Tax Credit (ITC) | The GST you already paid on your purchases, which you can subtract from what you owe. |
| Assessee | The person or business responsible for paying the tax. |
| Assessment Year (AY) | The year you file your return (which is the year after you earned the money). |
| Financial Year (FY) | The year you actually earned the money (April 1 to March 31). |
| Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) | Money kept aside from a payment to give directly to the government. |
| GSTIN | Your 15-digit unique GST registration number. |
| Scrutiny Assessment | The tax department demanding proof that your filed return is accurate. |
| Return Filing | Submitting a formal report of your income or sales to the government. |
| GSTR-1 | The monthly report of all the sales you made. |
| GSTR-3B | The monthly form where you actually calculate and pay your GST. |
| GSTR-2B | The auto-generated list of all purchases where your suppliers charged you GST. |
| Output Tax | The GST you collected from your customers. |
| Challan | The official receipt proving you deposited money into the government's bank. |
| Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) | A secure USB token used to digitally sign documents instead of using a pen. |
| Form 16 | The certificate your employer gives you showing how much tax they deducted. |
| Form 26AS | Your tax passbook showing all tax paid against your PAN by anyone. |
| PAN | Your permanent 10-digit ID used for everything tax-related. |
| TAN | The number you need if you are deducting tax from other people's payments. |
| Exempt Supply | Goods or services that the government has decided do not attract any GST. |
| Nil Rated | Items taxed at 0%, meaning no tax is added to the bill. |
| Zero Rated Supply | Exports; you don't charge tax, but you can claim back the tax you paid on expenses. |
| Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM) | When the buyer has to pay the GST directly to the government instead of the seller. |
| Show Cause Notice (SCN) | A formal letter asking you to explain why you shouldn't be penalized. |
| Advance Tax | Paying your income tax in installments throughout the year instead of all at once. |
| Section 80C | The most common rule that lets you reduce your taxable income by investing. |
| Capital Gains | The profit you make from selling an asset like property or stocks. |
| Tax Audit | A CA reviewing your books because your business turnover crossed a legal limit. |
| Depreciation | Reducing the taxable value of an asset as it gets older and loses value. |
| Set Off | Using your business losses from this year to reduce your tax bill next year. |
| Turnover | The total amount of money your business took in from sales before any expenses. |
| E-Way Bill | A digital permit required to transport goods worth more than ₹50,000. |
| HSN Code | An international numbering system to classify exactly what product you are selling. |
| SAC Code | The numbering system used to classify what service you are providing. |
| Composition Scheme | A simpler GST system for small businesses with fewer rules but no input tax credit. |
| Refund Claim | Asking the government to return tax money you overpaid. |
| Appellate Tribunal | The special court that handles tax disputes. |
| Condonation of Delay | Asking the department to forgive you for missing a deadline due to a genuine reason. |
| Ex Parte Order | The officer deciding your tax bill without you being present, usually because you ignored notices. |
| Demand Notice | A strict order telling you exactly how much money you owe and by when. |
| E-Invoicing | Generating your sales invoices directly through the government's portal. |
