# Input Tax Credit

Input Tax Credit allows businesses to reduce the tax they have already paid on purchases from the tax they owe on their sales. It prevents the cascading effect of taxes, ensuring tax is only paid on the value added.

### Example
You buy raw materials and pay ₹1,000 GST. You sell finished goods and owe ₹1,500 GST. Using ITC, you only pay the ₹500 difference.

### When It Matters in Practice
It matters when filing regular GST returns and determining the net tax liability to be paid in cash.
