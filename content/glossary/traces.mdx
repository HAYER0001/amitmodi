TDS Reconciliation Analysis and Correction Enabling System

TRACES is a web-based portal of the Income Tax Department that provides comprehensive facilities for the administration, reconciliation, and correction of Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) and Tax Collected at Source (TCS) returns.

### Example
A company's accountant logs into the TRACES portal to download the consolidated Form 16/16A for their employees and vendors after successfully filing the quarterly TDS returns.

### When it matters in practice
It matters for deductors to view challan status, download justification reports for defaults, and generate TDS certificates. For taxpayers, it allows viewing of Form 26AS to verify taxes deducted by others on their behalf.
