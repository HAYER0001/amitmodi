Certificate of Tax Deducted at Source on Income other than Salary

Form 16A is a TDS certificate issued by a deductor for tax deducted on incomes other than salary, such as interest earned on fixed deposits, rent receipts, insurance commissions, or professional fees paid to contractors.

### Example
A freelance consultant receives a Form 16A from a client showing that 10% TDS was deducted from their ₹100,000 professional fee and remitted to the government, which the consultant can claim as a tax credit.

### When it matters in practice
It is crucial for non-salaried individuals, freelancers, and businesses to verify the tax deducted on their various income streams, ensuring they receive appropriate credit for TDS when filing their annual tax returns.
