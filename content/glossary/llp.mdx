Full form: Limited Liability Partnership

An LLP is a hybrid corporate business vehicle that combines the flexible internal management of a traditional partnership with the limited liability protection of a company, safeguarding partners' personal assets from business debts.

### Example
Three chartered accountants start an advisory firm. They register an LLP. If the firm is sued for ₹50 Lakhs [VERIFY], the partners' personal savings are protected; their liability is limited to their agreed capital contribution in the LLP.

### When it matters in practice
It matters for professional services firms and small-to-medium businesses that want corporate status and limited liability without the heavy compliance burden of a private limited company.
