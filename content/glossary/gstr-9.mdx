Goods and Services Tax Return 9

GSTR-9 is the annual return mandated for regular GST-registered taxpayers, consolidating all monthly or quarterly returns (GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B) filed during the financial year. It serves as a comprehensive annual reconciliation of outward supplies, inward supplies, taxes paid, and ITC claimed.

### Worked Example
A manufacturing company compiles data from its 12 monthly GSTR-3B and GSTR-1 filings for FY 2023-24 to file its GSTR-9, correcting any discrepancies or missed ITC claims before the annual deadline.
