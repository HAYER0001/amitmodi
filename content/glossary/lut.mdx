# Letter of Undertaking

A Letter of Undertaking is a document filed by an exporter to export goods or services without paying Integrated Goods and Services Tax (IGST). It acts as a commitment that export rules will be followed.

### Example
An IT firm exports software to the US. By filing an LUT, they export the services with zero IGST payment upfront instead of paying and claiming a refund.

### When It Matters in Practice
Essential for exporters wanting to avoid cash blockage. Valid for one financial year.
