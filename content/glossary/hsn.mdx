# Harmonized System of Nomenclature

The Harmonized System of Nomenclature is an internationally accepted, standardized system of names and numbers used to classify traded products. Under GST, it determines the applicable tax rate for goods.

### Example
You sell roasted coffee beans. You look up the HSN code 0901, which tells you the correct GST rate to charge on your invoice.

### When It Matters in Practice
Mandatory on B2B tax invoices. Small businesses with turnover up to <Verify>₹5 Crores</Verify> need a 4-digit code, others need 6 digits.
