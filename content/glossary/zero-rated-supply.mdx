**Zero-Rated Supply**

A zero-rated supply refers to goods or services that are exported or supplied to a Special Economic Zone (SEZ). These supplies are taxed at a 0% [VERIFY] rate, meaning the entire value chain is free from tax.

### Example
An Indian manufacturer exports software to a client in the USA. The export is a zero-rated supply, and the manufacturer can claim a refund for any taxes paid on inputs used to develop the software.

### When it matters
It matters for exporters and businesses supplying to SEZs, as they can either export without paying integrated tax under a Letter of Undertaking (LUT) or pay the tax and claim a refund.
