# Tax Collected at Source

Tax Collected at Source is an income tax mechanism where the seller collects an additional amount as tax from the buyer at the time of sale of specific goods (like vehicles, scrap, or timber) and deposits it with the government.

### Example
A dealer sells a luxury car for ₹15 Lakhs. They collect <Verify>1%</Verify> TCS from the buyer and remit it to the government.

### When It Matters in Practice
Applies to specific goods and transactions exceeding a set threshold (e.g., motor vehicles over <Verify>₹10 Lakhs</Verify>).
