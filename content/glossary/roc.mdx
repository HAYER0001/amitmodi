Registrar of Companies

The Registrar of Companies (ROC) is an office under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) that handles the administration of companies and LLPs in a specific state or jurisdiction, including their registration, compliance tracking, and striking off.

### Example
If a company based in Mumbai fails to file its annual returns for consecutive years, the ROC Mumbai can issue a notice and potentially strike off the company's name from the register.

### When it matters in practice
It matters for state-specific corporate compliance. Any change in the company's registered office, directors, or capital structure must be filed with and approved by the respective ROC where the company is registered.
