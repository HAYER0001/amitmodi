# Tax Deduction and Collection Account Number

Tax Deduction and Collection Account Number is a unique ten-digit alphanumeric number issued by the Income Tax Department to individuals or businesses who are responsible for deducting or collecting tax at source on behalf of the government.

### Example
A company pays a contractor ₹1,00,000. They must deduct TDS and deposit it. To do this, they need a TAN.

### When It Matters in Practice
Mandatory for all entities deducting TDS or collecting TCS. Penalty of <Verify>₹10,000</Verify> applies for failing to quote TAN.
