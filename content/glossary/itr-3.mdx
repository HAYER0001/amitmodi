Income Tax Return Form 3

ITR-3 is the tax return form for individuals and HUFs generating income from a proprietary business or profession. It encompasses all heads of income, including business profits, salary, house property, and capital gains, requiring detailed financial statements and audit compliance.

### Worked Example
A freelance software developer earning ₹40 Lakhs from clients and ₹2 Lakhs from stock market trading (capital gains) must file ITR-3 to report both professional income and capital gains.

### When it matters in practice
It matters for any individual running a proprietorship, working as a freelancer, or practicing a profession (like doctors, lawyers) where they maintain books of accounts and possibly require a tax audit if turnover exceeds ₹1 Crore [VERIFY threshold].
