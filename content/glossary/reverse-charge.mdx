**Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM)**

Under the Reverse Charge Mechanism, the liability to pay GST is shifted from the supplier of goods or services to the recipient. This ensures tax collection where the supplier is unregistered or provides specified services.

### Example
If a registered business hires a goods transport agency (GTA) that does not pay GST, the business must pay the GST directly to the government under RCM instead of paying it to the GTA.

### When it matters
It is critical when dealing with unregistered suppliers, importing services, or using specific services like legal services or transport. Failing to pay RCM taxes can result in penalties [VERIFY].
