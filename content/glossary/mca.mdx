Ministry of Corporate Affairs

The Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) is an Indian government portal and regulatory body responsible for regulating corporate affairs through the Companies Act, 2013 and the Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008, overseeing company incorporation, compliance, and governance.

### Example
To register a new Private Limited Company, founders must submit their SPICe+ incorporation forms online through the MCA V3 portal.

### When it matters in practice
It is the central hub for all corporate compliance; every company and LLP must interact with the MCA portal to file annual returns, change directors, update registered addresses, and file resolutions.
