Income Tax Return Form 7

ITR-7 is the income tax return form specifically designed for persons including companies required to furnish return under sections 139(4A), 139(4B), 139(4C), or 139(4D). It primarily serves charitable trusts, political parties, research associations, and educational institutions claiming tax exemptions.

### Worked Example
A registered charitable trust managing an educational institution and claiming tax exemption under Section 11 must file its annual income and expenditure details using ITR-7.
