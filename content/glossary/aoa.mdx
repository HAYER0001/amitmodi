Full form: Articles of Association

The AOA is the internal rulebook of a company that governs its day-to-day management, outlines the rights, duties, and powers of directors and shareholders, and dictates procedures for issuing shares, holding meetings, and paying dividends.

### Example
A Private Limited Company's AOA contains a restrictive clause [VERIFY] stating that shares cannot be sold to an outsider unless they are first offered to existing shareholders, ensuring the founders retain tight control over ownership.

### When it matters in practice
It matters extensively when drafting shareholder agreements, resolving internal disputes, or structuring board powers, as the AOA legally binds the company and its members to follow established internal procedures.
