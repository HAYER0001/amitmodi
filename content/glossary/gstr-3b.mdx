Goods and Services Tax Return 3B

GSTR-3B is a self-declared summary GST return filed monthly or quarterly, consolidating total sales, ITC claimed, and the net tax payable. It is the form through which a taxpayer actually pays the final GST liability to the government for the given tax period.

### Worked Example
A business reports ₹50,000 as output tax liability on sales and claims ₹30,000 as ITC on purchases in GSTR-3B, paying the net ₹20,000 tax liability in cash.
