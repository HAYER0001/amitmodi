# Reverse Charge Mechanism

Reverse Charge Mechanism is a scenario under GST where the recipient of the goods or services is liable to pay the tax directly to the government instead of the supplier, shifting the typical tax liability.

### Example
A registered business hires a goods transport agency. Under RCM, the business (recipient) pays the GST directly rather than the transporter.

### When It Matters in Practice
It applies to specific services like transport, legal, and sponsorship.
