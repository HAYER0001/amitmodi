**Exempt Supply**

An exempt supply under GST is a supply of goods or services that attracts a nil rate of tax or is wholly exempt from tax. Importantly, businesses cannot claim Input Tax Credit on inputs used for exempt supplies.

### Example
Fresh milk, unbranded flour, and certain agricultural products are exempt from GST. A dairy farmer selling fresh milk does not charge GST and cannot claim input credit on the cattle feed.

### When it matters
It matters when determining a business's aggregate turnover for GST registration (threshold is ₹20 Lakhs or ₹40 Lakhs [VERIFY]) and when calculating proportionate Input Tax Credit reversals.
