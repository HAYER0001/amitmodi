**Assessment Year (AY)**

The Assessment Year is the 12-month period starting on April 1 [VERIFY] during which a taxpayer’s income earned in the immediately preceding financial year is evaluated, taxed, and the income tax return is filed.

### Example
Income earned between April 1, 2023, and March 31, 2024 (Financial Year 2023-24), will be assessed and taxed in the Assessment Year 2024-25, starting from April 1, 2024.

### When it matters
It is essential when filing Income Tax Returns (ITR), as the forms and tax rules apply to a specific AY. Deadlines, such as July 31 [VERIFY] for individuals, are tracked by the AY.
