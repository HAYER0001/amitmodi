Electronic Verification Code

An Electronic Verification Code (EVC) is a 10-digit alphanumeric code sent to a taxpayer's registered mobile number and email ID, used to electronically verify Income Tax Returns and other forms without needing a physical signature or Digital Signature Certificate.

### Example
After filing her ITR-1 on the e-filing portal, Priya selects the "e-Verify using Aadhaar OTP" option. She receives an EVC on her phone, enters it online, and instantly completes her return verification.

### When it matters in practice
It is highly convenient for individual taxpayers and small entities to instantly authenticate tax returns, avoiding the hassle of sending a physically signed ITR-V to the Centralized Processing Center (CPC) in Bengaluru.
