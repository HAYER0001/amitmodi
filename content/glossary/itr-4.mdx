Income Tax Return Form 4 (Sugam)

ITR-4 is a simplified tax form for individuals, HUFs, and partnership firms opting for the presumptive taxation scheme. It allows eligible taxpayers to declare income at a prescribed percentage of their gross receipts, eliminating the need to maintain detailed books of accounts.

### Worked Example
A retail shop owner with an annual turnover of ₹80 Lakhs opts for Section 44AD and declares an 8% profit margin (₹6.4 Lakhs), filing ITR-4 without maintaining detailed ledgers.

### When it matters in practice
It matters for small businesses and professionals aiming to reduce compliance burdens, provided their total turnover is below ₹3 Crores for business [VERIFY threshold] or ₹75 Lakhs for professionals [VERIFY threshold] under the presumptive scheme.
