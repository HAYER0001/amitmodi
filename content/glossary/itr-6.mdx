Income Tax Return Form 6

ITR-6 is the dedicated tax return form for all companies registered under the Companies Act, excluding those claiming exemption under Section 11 (charitable/religious trusts). It necessitates detailed corporate financial disclosures, balance sheets, and compliance with the Minimum Alternate Tax (MAT).

### Worked Example
A Private Limited Company engaged in manufacturing must electronically file ITR-6, appending its audited balance sheet, profit and loss account, and auditor's report for the financial year.

### When it matters in practice
It is critical for corporate compliance; every registered company must file ITR-6 irrespective of profit or loss, typically by October 31st [VERIFY deadline] following the financial year, to avoid substantial late filing penalties.
