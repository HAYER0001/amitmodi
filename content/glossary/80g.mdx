Full form: Section 80G of the Income Tax Act, 1961

80G registration allows donors who contribute to registered NGOs and charitable trusts to claim a tax deduction on their donations, encouraging philanthropy by reducing the donor's overall taxable income.

### Example
An individual donates ₹50,000 [VERIFY] to an 80G-registered NGO. The donor can claim a deduction of 50% [VERIFY] (i.e., ₹25,000 [VERIFY]) of the donated amount from their gross total income, subject to a qualifying limit of 10% [VERIFY] of adjusted gross total income.

### When it matters in practice
It is crucial for NGOs during fundraising, as donors strongly prefer contributing to organizations where they can receive tax benefits for their donations.
