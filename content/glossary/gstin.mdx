# Goods and Services Tax Identification Number

A Goods and Services Tax Identification Number is a unique 15-digit alphanumeric identifier assigned to every registered taxpayer under the GST regime in India, replacing the old system of multiple registration numbers.

### Example
A business in Maharashtra is issued GSTIN 27AAAAA0000A1Z5 upon registration, used for all GST compliance and invoicing.

### When It Matters in Practice
Mandatory for businesses exceeding the turnover threshold of <Verify>₹40 Lakhs</Verify> (goods) or <Verify>₹20 Lakhs</Verify> (services).
