## 1. GST Compliance Checklist (Monthly & Annual)
**Title:** The Ultimate GST Compliance Checklist for Growing Businesses
**Subtitle:** Never miss a deadline, reverse an ITC claim, or pay a late fee again.

### Monthly Routine (Before the 10th of every month)
- [ ] **Reconcile Sales Register:** Ensure all B2B and B2C sales are recorded with correct GSTINs.
- [ ] **GSTR-2B Reconciliation:** Download GSTR-2B and match it against your purchase register.
- [ ] **Flag Missing ITC:** Identify vendors who haven't filed their GSTR-1 and follow up.
- [ ] **Check RCM Liabilities:** Calculate Reverse Charge Mechanism liabilities (e.g., freight, legal services, sponsorships).
- [ ] **Generate e-Invoices:** Ensure all B2B invoices above the ₹5 Crore threshold have an IRN and QR code.

### Filing Sequence
- [ ] **By 11th:** File GSTR-1 (Outward Supplies).
- [ ] **By 13th:** File IFF (if opted for QRMP scheme).
- [ ] **By 20th:** File GSTR-3B (Summary Return) and pay tax liability.

### Annual Compliance
- [ ] **Verify Rule 42/43 Reversals:** Re-calculate exempt vs taxable supply ITC reversals.
- [ ] **File GSTR-9:** Annual return (Mandatory if turnover > ₹2 Crore).
- [ ] **File GSTR-9C:** Reconciliation statement (Mandatory if turnover > ₹5 Crore).

*Disclaimer: This checklist is for informational purposes only. Consult your tax professional for your specific requirements.*

## 2. Document Checklist Pack for 8 Core Services
**Title:** The Master Document Pack: What You Need Before You Apply

### 1. GST Registration
- PAN Card (Proprietor/Company)
- Aadhaar Card (Proprietor/Directors)
- Proof of Business Address (Electricity Bill + NOC / Rent Agreement)
- Cancelled Cheque / Bank Statement
- Board Resolution (for Companies)

### 2. Entity Formation (Private Limited Company)
- PAN & Aadhaar of all Directors
- Passport Size Photographs
- Latest Bank Statement / Utility Bill (Personal)
- Registered Office Proof (Electricity Bill + NOC)
- Proposed Company Names (Minimum 2)

### 3. Income Tax Returns
- Form 16 / 16A / 16B / 16C
- Bank Statements (April 1 to March 31)
- Form 26AS & Annual Information Statement (AIS)
- Investment Proofs (80C, 80D, etc.)
- Home Loan Interest Certificate

### 4. TDS Returns
- TAN Certificate
- Deductee PAN Details
- Challan details (ITNS 281) for TDS deposited
- Invoice copies for deductions made under 194C, 194J, 194I, etc.

### 5. Import Export Code (IEC) & LUT
- PAN Card of Entity
- Cancelled Cheque
- Class 3 Digital Signature (DSC)
- Board Resolution / Partnership Deed
- Address Proof of Firm

### 6. Trust Registration (12A & 80G)
- Registered Trust Deed / Society Registration Certificate
- PAN Card of the Trust
- List of Trustees with KYC
- Last 3 years Audited Financials (if applicable)
- Note on Charitable Activities

### 7. Tax Appeals (CIT-A)
- Copy of the Assessment Order / Notice
- Notice of Demand (Section 156)
- Grounds of Appeal & Statement of Facts
- Challan for Appeal Fee Paid
- Proof of 20% pre-deposit (if applicable)

### 8. PAN Card / TAN Registration
- Identity Proof (Aadhaar/Voter ID)
- Address Proof (Utility Bill / Passport)
- Date of Birth Proof (Birth Certificate / 10th Marksheet)
- 2 Passport Size Photos (for Individuals)

## 3. Annual Compliance Calendar
**Title:** The Complete Statutory Calendar for Indian Businesses (Financial Year)

### Every Month
- **7th:** Deposit TDS/TCS for the previous month.
- **11th:** File GSTR-1 (for monthly filers).
- **15th:** Issue Provident Fund (PF) and ESI payments.
- **20th:** File GSTR-3B (for monthly filers) and pay GST.

### Quarterly Deadlines
- **15th of June, Sept, Dec, March:** Advance Tax Instalments due (15%, 45%, 75%, 100%).
- **31st of July, Oct, Jan, May:** File quarterly TDS Returns (Form 24Q, 26Q).
- **13th of month following quarter:** File GSTR-1 (QRMP scheme).
- **22nd/24th of month following quarter:** File GSTR-3B (QRMP scheme).

### Annual Milestones
- **May 31st:** Issue Form 16 to employees.
- **July 31st:** ITR Filing for non-audit individuals.
- **Sept 30th:** Audit Report filing (Tax Audit).
- **Oct 31st:** ITR Filing for audit cases and companies.
- **Dec 31st:** File GSTR-9 and GSTR-9C for the previous financial year.
