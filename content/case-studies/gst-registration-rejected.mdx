## Situation
A retail textile trader based in Surat with an annual turnover of approximately ₹4 crore had attempted to apply for GST registration. Their application was rejected twice by the jurisdictional officer due to alleged discrepancies in the principal place of business address proofs and mismatch in the Aadhaar authentication details.

## Task
The client required a valid GST Identification Number (GSTIN) within seven days to clear a pending commercial shipment and legally collect tax from B2B buyers. The prior rejections needed to be addressed and rectified in a fresh, error-free application.

## Action
1. **Document Audit:** We reviewed the previous rejection notices (Form GST REG-03) and identified that the rental agreement lacked the required notary seal, and the electricity bill was in the landlord's spouse's name.
2. **Clarification Gathering:** We drafted a formal No Objection Certificate (NOC) and had it duly executed by the legal owner of the premises. 
3. **Application Filing:** We filed a fresh application via Form GST REG-01, uploading the corrected, high-resolution address proofs along with the NOC.
4. **Jurisdictional Follow-up:** We actively tracked the application status and provided timely clarification to the reviewing officer.

## Result
The GST registration was approved without further queries. The client received their GSTIN within 4 working days of our submission, allowing them to release their blocked shipments and commence B2B invoicing immediately.
