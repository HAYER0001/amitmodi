## Situation
A newly formed educational charitable trust in Bangalore, running a primary school for underprivileged children, needed to apply for tax exemption. They required registration under Section 12A to exempt their own income from tax, and Section 80G to allow their donors to claim tax deductions on their contributions.

## Task
The trust required provisional registration under both sections simultaneously to launch their first major fundraising campaign before the financial year ended.

## Action
1. **Deed Review:** We reviewed the registered trust deed to ensure it contained the mandatory irrevocability clause and explicit statements that the trust's activities were strictly charitable and not for the benefit of any specific religious community or caste.
2. **Form Preparation:** We compiled the required three years of projected financials, a detailed note on proposed charitable activities, and the PAN details of all managing trustees.
3. **Filing Form 10A:** We filed Form 10A on the Income Tax e-filing portal, selecting the appropriate codes for provisional registration under Section 12A(1)(ac)(vi) and Section 80G(5)(iv).
4. **Compliance Assurance:** We set up an internal calendar for the trust to ensure they applied for regular registration within six months of commencing activities.

## Result
Provisional registration for both Section 12A and Section 80G was successfully granted and the certificates were issued. The trust launched their fundraising campaign on schedule, fully compliant with the Income Tax Act.
