## Situation
A construction contractor in Delhi approached us after realizing they had deducted TDS from subcontractors and employees for three consecutive financial years but had failed to file the quarterly TDS returns (Form 24Q and 26Q). They were facing severe daily late filing fees under Section 234E and interest on late deposits.

## Task
We needed to quantify the exact statutory liability (including tax, interest, and late fees), deposit the pending amounts, file 12 pending quarterly returns, and halt the ongoing accumulation of the ₹200/day penalty.

## Action
1. **Liability Assessment:** We extracted three years of general ledgers and salary registers. We calculated the exact TDS to be deducted under sections 194C, 194J, and 192.
2. **Challan Generation & Payment:** We computed the penal interest at 1.5% per month for the delayed deposit of tax. We then generated the required challans (ITNS 281) and instructed the client to deposit the total tax and interest immediately.
3. **Late Fee Computation & Return Preparation:** We calculated the mandatory late fee under Section 234E (capped at the TDS amount) and prepared the FVU files for all 12 quarters using the paid challan details.
4. **Filing & Issuance:** We successfully uploaded the returns to the TRACES portal. Once processed, we downloaded and issued Form 16 and Form 16A to all deductees.

## Result
All pending TDS returns were successfully filed, stopping the daily accumulation of penalties. The business became fully compliant, allowing their subcontractors to finally claim their pending tax credits.
