## Situation
A software development agency in Hyderabad secured its first overseas client in the United States. The firm needed to export IT services but lacked the necessary regulatory registrations, exposing them to the risk of having to pay 18% IGST on their export invoices unnecessarily.

## Task
The client needed an Import Export Code (IEC) from the DGFT and a Letter of Undertaking (LUT) under GST to legally export services without payment of Integrated GST (IGST). Both were required within two weeks to issue their first international invoice.

## Action
1. **IEC Registration:** We gathered the company's PAN, bank certificate, and incorporation documents. We filed the application on the DGFT portal, ensuring the correct NIC code for software services was selected.
2. **GST LUT Filing:** Once the IEC was issued, we prepared and filed Form GST RFD-11 on the GST portal to furnish the Letter of Undertaking for the current financial year. We ensured two independent witnesses' details were correctly verified and attached.
3. **Invoicing Advisory:** We provided the client with a compliant export invoice template, strictly including the mandatory endorsement: "Supply meant for export under bond or letter of undertaking without payment of integrated tax."

## Result
The IEC was issued in 2 days and the LUT was successfully accepted on the GST portal. The client issued their first international invoice without charging 18% IGST, maintaining compliance and protecting their cash flow.
